Chips

Your Wacky Bucks are physical chips in five colours: 1, 5, 25, 100 and 500. Pick a chip, click a betting area to place it, and winnings are paid back onto the felt before they're swept into your rack. Your chips are saved between visits.

Table games

  • Blackjack: get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Blackjack pays 3:2, and the dealer stands on all 17s. You can double on your first two cards and split pairs.
  • Roulette: bet on numbers, colours, odd/even, dozens and more, then spin. A straight-up number pays 35:1.
  • Baccarat: bet on the Player, the Banker or a Tie, and the hand closest to 9 wins. Banker wins pay 0.95:1 and a Tie pays 8:1.
  • Casino Hold'em: place an Ante and see your two cards plus the flop. Then Call (2× the Ante) or fold. Your best five cards are compared with the dealer's, and the dealer needs a pair of 4s or better to qualify.
  • Three Card Poker: an Ante and/or Pair Plus bet. Play (1× the Ante) to beat the dealer, who needs Queen-high to qualify. Pair Plus pays on your hand alone.
  • Wacky Derby: six horses race across the table. Win pays 5:1 and Top 3 pays 1:1.

Floor games

  • Plinko: drop a ball and it lands on a multiplier at the bottom.
  • Big Wheel: bet on the symbol you think the wheel will stop on.
  • Slots: the classic machines are three-reel games. The video slots (fishing, tomb and cosmic themes) have 20 paylines, free spins, pick bonuses, hold & spin and a gamble feature.

Talk to a bartender for a drink. At the glass-topped sports tables, bet on the home or away side of tiny football, basketball and ice hockey matches (football also takes Draw bets). Odds are shown before kick-off.
